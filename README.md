# Research Analyst | Data Science & Applied Analytics

## About Me

I'm a research and data analytics professional with experience applying data science, statistical analysis, and business intelligence to challenges in higher education and community-based organizations. My work focuses on transforming complex data into actionable insights through exploratory analysis, predictive modeling, performance measurement, and interactive dashboards.
